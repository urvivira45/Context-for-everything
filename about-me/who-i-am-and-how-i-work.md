## Writing style rules (everything I publish)
- No em dashes.
- Plain, specific, honest. No flowery or poetic language.
- Human voice with depth, not AI-sounding patterns.
- No choppy fragments. Proper-length sentences with commas where needed.
- Instagram captions: the first 3 to 4 words must be the hook or CTA.
