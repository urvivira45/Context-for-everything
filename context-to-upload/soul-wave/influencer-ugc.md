# Soul Wave Influencer and UGC Campaign

Last updated: 12 Nov 2026. From the influencer chat.

## Purpose
- Goal: UGC, trust and talk across India. Sales from the campaign itself are not the target.
- Barter only, gifted kits to micro-influencers. 10 to 20 kits affordable.
- Everyone approached as one group. Ad rights are a bonus, not a requirement. Not raising website embeds with creators.

## Creator categories
- Busy professionals with an after-work reset (replaced Mumbai-local).
- Parents buying for their kids (not doing it together).
- New chapter (new mothers) and second innings (retired, empty nest) as separate categories.
- Kids category postponed.

## Content rules
- No anti-screen or anti-Netflix framing from the brand, no "mindful", no problem-led openings, no health claims, no em dashes.
- Screen time allowed only in a creator's own voice, one creator at most, plus once in a parent context.
- No "grandma hobbies" (condescending to 60+ buyers). No "I almost didn't order this" (false for gifted kits, legal risk).

## Features every brief uses
1. Everything you need to start is in the kit.
2. Beginner support: video tutorial plus WhatsApp support (emphasis varies by reel).
Each brief states its feature as a sentence, not a tag. Daily reminder stays dropped. "Finished piece" and "habit" are outcomes, not features.

## Brief format
- The idea, one layered hook (two parts used together, no labels), and a note to replace the hook with something that stops their own scroll.
- Non-negotiables: natural to how you talk; visible in frame at some point; at least two process moments; on-screen text (most watch on mute); mention Soul Wave once; don't show contact details on the support card or tutorial QR.
- One inspiration reel per creator, sent in the 2nd or 3rd message, framed as tone, not to copy.

## Outreach
- Same warm first DM for everyone (mom and couple variants written). No em dashes, no "mindfully".
- Sequence: hi → idea → dispatch → videos for approval → they post as a collab.
- No memorandum for everyone. Ad rights asked only where an ad is planned.
- Ad angles (if used): WhatsApp support, unboxing, Rakhi gifting, after-work story.

## 8 concepts (pending my final confirmation)
Talking unboxing / Musical unboxing ("I need to order something for my mental peace / the order:") / "I can't believe I made this" / Got stuck, someone helped / Date night / Slow-flow day / 5 to 9 after my 9 to 5 / New hobby, addictive.

## Open
- Confirm the 8 concepts, assign creators, write 8 final briefs, send DMs, set approval criteria and the post-posting plan.
- Wider plan: 50 creator collabs (10 old kits, 40 new). Creators are for buzz only, never counted in sales forecasts.
