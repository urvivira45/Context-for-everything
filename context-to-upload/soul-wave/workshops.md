## Ads
- Two ads in use: Ad 1 "POV: hours that actually feel like yours" (offer hook, names the workshop at second zero, proven converter) and Ad 2 "3 reasons to try Punch Needle with Soul Wave" (why us, not YouTube).
- Always pair an offer-hook ad with a content ad. Never two content ads together.
- Five primary texts per ad, swapped per venue. Headlines "Limited Seats" or "Grab Your Seat". End card: Joining us? / Punch Needle Workshop / date / venue.
- Next ad comes from whichever reel outperforms organically.
- Early setup rules: Advantage+ placements off, manual Reels/Stories, ~5km radius, craft interests + top-10% income, WhatsApp objective.
- Budget pattern used: ~₹1,400 per location (₹164/day x 7 days + GST), re-message existing leads first.
- Use only our own samples and photos in ads, never another creator's.

## WhatsApp flow
- Cold leads: greeting that lets them pick a location (Meta mixes areas), location details with map link, founder intro, voice note for first-timers, advance ask, confirmation, day-before reminder, morning-of. Quick replies /2M /3V /4T style.
- Follow-up order: testimonial video (mother-daughter one performs best), participant artwork, Google review, room shot, direct ask with seat count, soft close asking locality.
- Cadence: cold leads daily, warm alternate days.
- The permission pattern ("shall I...", "will it be okay if...") is core. Never tighten it out.
- Registration via Tally form; go/no-go rule before each date.

## Nov to Dec 2026 season

## Lessons
- No weekday workshops (Malad Friday failed, Saturday fixed it).
- Need ~2 weeks lead time, not 5 days.
- Repeat venues are cheaper than new ones.
- Long weekends and festivals kill attendance (2 to 4 Oct).
- Conversion is the bottleneck, not traffic (100+ chats, 5 registrations in Oct).
- CAC: ₹167 Aug, ₹228 average now, target ₹200.
