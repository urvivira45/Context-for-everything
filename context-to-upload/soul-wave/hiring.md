# Soul Wave Hiring: Content Creation Associates

Last updated: 12 Nov 2026. From the hiring chat.

## Structure
- Two entry-level hires, 3-month paid pilot (not an internship) converting to full-time with a raise.
- Budget ₹20k/month total. Pilot pay ₹8,000 to 10,000 each; full-time ₹12,000 to 18,000.
- Both in office, Lower Parel.

## Roles
- **Shooting:** Reels, Shorts, ad footage and photos for Soul Wave and my personal brand. Shot setup from reference reels and shot lists, posting per calendar, logging numbers. Joins workshops within Mumbai when needed.
- **Editing:** edits Reels and ads in VN, repurposes footage, song and audio research, file organisation, posting per calendar, logging numbers.
- Both later suggest content ideas and help with first messages to creators using preset messaging.

## Terms
- Hours 11:00 AM to 7:30 PM, 30-min lunch, 15-min break.
- Alternating six- and five-day weeks: every Sunday off, alternate Saturdays off.
- Weekend workshop → comp-off that week.
- Train pass from their nearest station to the office's, nothing else.

## JD rules
- No "Lala company" lines, no "expert from day one", no promise to teach upfront.
- Need an eye for short-form, not expertise. Pay first under "What you'll get". Human but professional.
- No health claims, not a "digital detox" brand. Two PDFs in Soul Wave colours. Applications to soulwavekits@gmail.com.

## Process
- Invite email (asks for a call slot, no task mention) → confirmation with Google Meet link → 20 to 30 min call → task email right after → submit by 6 PM next day, I reply within 24 hours → decline or office-visit acceptance (timings, leave, pay, joining date).
- Tasks (evaluation only, never used): Shooting: 6 to 8 vertical clips of "a calm moment from your day", shot list, 3 lines on the reference. Editing: 20 to 30 sec video on the same theme from own or stock clips, following a reference reel, plus audio choice and 3 lines.
- Scoring out of 40 (20 call, 20 task): 32+ offer, 26 to 31 maybe, under 26 decline. Reliability/fit scored 1 = decline regardless.
- Never ask about marital status, children, religion, caste, health or age.
