# Meta Ads: Campaign 1 Facts and Campaign 2 Plan

Last updated: 12 Nov 2026. From the Meta ads advisor chat. This is the plan as of ~Oct 11. What actually ran and its results are NOT recorded yet.

## Campaign 1 (WhatsApp-chat funnel)
- Spent ₹8,474 for 1,186 chats and 10 confirmed sales (9 ads, 1 friend). Cold CAC ~₹941. Meta claimed 12 purchases.
- Chats at ₹6.5 to 8 each did not turn into buyers. Cities of leads were not recorded.

## Campaign 2 decisions
- Traffic to the website instead of WhatsApp.
- Targeting: ₹1 lakh line = household affordability, so pincode targeting, not all India. Prefer old-money, owner-heavy pincodes; count housing costs against disposable income (why Mumbai was discounted).
- October set (one ad set, kept wide): Chennai, Coimbatore, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Thiruvananthapuram. 58 verified pincodes (36 core, 22 extended) in workbook `soulwave_pincodes_merged.xlsx`.
