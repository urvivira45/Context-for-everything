# Soul Wave: Finance (CFO chat)

Last updated: 17 Nov 2026

Strategy, pricing history and roadmap live in `strategy-and-finance.md` (CEO chat). This file holds the CFO chat decisions.

Source of numbers: Google Sheet "Soul Wave Finances & Inventory". Share it with Claude as a downloaded .xlsx so every tab comes through.

## How the sheet is read
- Three layers: **Pricing Per Unit** = real per-unit cost from actual purchases. **Pricing Strategy** = planning cost with buffers, used for pricing. **All the Expenses So far** = the actual cash ledger.
- Money already spent (in the running total) is kept separate from money still to spend, so nothing is counted twice.
- Fabric + printing for the first kit prints (₹2,000 + ₹750 = ₹2,750) is already paid.
- Ad spend has always been recorded GST-inclusive (workshop CAC, Campaign 1's ₹10,000).

## Cost rules (decided)
- A kit is always costed at its full cost, cloth and printing included, even when prints were prepaid.
- Packaging stays at ₹40 per kit (not the ₹36.56 from the packaging tab).
- GST + Razorpay combined at ₹100 per kit as a buffer. Not split. No Razorpay cost on workshops (cash/UPI).
- Shipping kept at ₹100 per kit as a buffer, even though Shadowfax is ₹59 to ₹69 + 18% GST.
- Needle costed at ₹90 and hoop at ₹25 in the sheet (current buy price ₹80 and ₹24.5; aiming lower on 100-piece orders). Per-unit cost uses the average of all purchases.
- Per order before ads: single costs ₹477 (keep ₹522 at ₹999), combo costs ₹681 (keep ₹668 at ₹1,349), 3 kits cost ₹853.
- Recurring costs, bought when stock runs out: kit prints (cloth + printing), workshop needles, hoops, fabric, yarn, felt; coaster workshop beads, coasters, wax pencils.
- Glue is no longer given in workshops. Leftover B-7000 stock goes to content.

## Kit campaign: 100 orders (decided 19 Oct)
- Cold start from the website, no warm leads, no workshop help counted.
- Ad ceiling ₹30,000 GST-inclusive for 100 orders (₹300 per order). Stop at the ceiling even if under 100 orders.
- Influencer gifting: 8 combos from old stock (16 prints), about ₹4,250, outside the ad budget.
- Inventory for the campaign (hoops, needles, yarn, cards, stickers) is bought up front. Already inside per-kit cost, so it is a timing cost, not an extra cost.
- 3 new designs added before ads, same cost and price, 50 prints per design. Reprint designs that sell well.
- Combos pushed first.
- CAC rule: ₹400 is the do-not-cross line; work in the ₹300 to ₹350 band.
