# Soul Wave Influencer and UGC Campaign (Campaign 2 collabs)

Last updated: 13 Nov 2026. From the influencer chat (June to Oct 2026).

## Purpose
- Goal: UGC, trust and talk across India. Fine if the campaign itself brings zero sales.
- Budget near zero: barter only, gifted kits to micro-influencers. 10 to 20 kits affordable for this round.
- No need to spend ₹5k to ₹10k on paid UGC. Collab reels go on the Soul Wave Instagram (as collabs) and are embedded on the website as trust proof ("we are not bluffing").
- Everyone approached as one group. Ad rights are a bonus if a creator agrees, not a requirement (previously: separate ad creators with rights asked upfront). Website embeds not raised with creators.
- Creators are for buzz only, never counted in sales forecasts. Wider plan: 50 creator collabs (10 on old kits, 40 on new).

## Creator categories (who)
- Busy working professional, after-work reset (replaced Mumbai-local, which was dropped).
- Parent buying for their kid (year-round buying, not gifting; not forced "do it together"). Kid category postponed to a later round.
- New season of life: new mother.
- Second innings: retired or empty nest, now has time for a hobby (separate from new mother; workshops show this crowd turns up).
- Couple.
- Students and Gen Z.
- Slow-living / self-care.
- Craft and DIY kept but weighted for credibility, not sales (price-sensitive, bargain-leaning).

## Features each reel highlights (one per reel, written as a sentence in the brief)
1. Everything you need to start Punch Needle is in the kit (not "everything", not "so much inside").
2. Beginner support: video tutorial plus WhatsApp support, emphasis varying by reel.
- Daily reminder stays dropped. "Finished piece" and "habit" are not features. Habit here only means adopting a hobby.
- Finished piece matters for home-decor buyers and makers who want to sell; show the maker holding it, not a flatlay.

## Content rules
- Lead with the joy of making. No anti-screen or anti-Netflix framing from the brand, no "mindful", no problem-led openings, no health claims.
- Screen time allowed in a creator's own voice (max one or two creators) and in the parent/kid context. Bending the bible here is a deliberate, one-off call.
- No overclaiming: not "the only thing", not "ultimate", no kid "asks to do it again". Date night: no "no dressing up".
- No "grandma hobbies" (condescending to 60+ buyers). No "I almost didn't order this" (creator got it free, legal risk).
- Hobby angle is "I'm allowed to take a break", not "a hobby just for me" (reads selfish in India).
- Say "feed", not "FYP". No skit/comedy hooks yet. Friendship Day skipped (not a gifting occasion in India).
- Gift angle is receiving ("someone gifted me this"), not giving.
- Commas, not em dashes, anywhere in briefs.

## Brief format (same for every brief)
- Short idea explanation with the feature woven in as a sentence.
- One layered hook (two parts used together, no "spoken" / "on-screen" labels), followed by: "Please replace this with something that would actually stop your scroll, you know your audience and the current Indian market."
- Non-negotiables (bullets): keep it natural to how you talk; be visible in frame at some point; at least two process moments (setting up, threading, punching); on-screen text since most watch on mute; mention Soul Wave once; don't show contact details on the support card or tutorial QR.
- Ideas are a direction with full creative freedom, not a script.
- One inspiration reel per creator (art-related, from creators she rates), sent in the 2nd or 3rd message, framed as tone, not to copy.

## Outreach
- Same warm first DM for everyone (mom and couple variants written), no ideas in it, no em dashes, no "mindfully".
- Sequence: hi, then idea, then dispatch, then videos for approval, then they post as a collab.
- No memorandum for everyone.
- Ad angles if rights come through: WhatsApp support, unboxing, Rakhi gifting, after-work story (narrowed from 7 to 6 to 4). Rakhi done as proper Rakhi gifting, not generic.

Bar: concepts must be trendy, scroll-stopping and worth visiting the website, not fluff. More collabs to follow after these 8.
