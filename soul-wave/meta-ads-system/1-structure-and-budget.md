# 1. Structure and Budget

Last updated: 23 Nov 2026.

## Rules (LOCKED)
- Cold traffic only; the website carries trust (reviews, videos).
- No views or awareness campaign on Meta; no retargeting of video viewers. Influencer work is separate and not counted for sales.
- All budgets and CAC include GST. CAC range: ₹300 (target) to ₹380 (ceiling).
- Few ads with enough budget each, not many ads on a thin budget.
- Never edit a working ad set. A new idea goes in a NEW campaign with its own budget (a new ad set inside an ABO campaign is acceptable; never inside a working CBO).
- Exclude Mumbai (workshops cover it). South first, North added later. Don't let narrow pincodes push CAC up.
- Audience Network always excluded.
- Partnership ads start in week 2 as their own campaign.
- Ads written as AIDA or PAS.

## Launch setup (PROPOSED, Claude recommends)
- One campaign, Sales, optimise for Purchase, ~₹2,000/day, ONE ad set: broad (target states, Mumbai excluded, age 25+, Advantage+ audience, no interests), 4 to 5 ads. Pincode list kept as a later test campaign (the budget can only feed one ad set: ₹14,000/week vs ₹19,000 needed at ₹380).
- Attribution: standard (7-day click, 1-day view).

## Stacking (PROPOSED)
- Launch the next campaign (new angle, offer, region or format, own budget) only when the running one holds CAC at ₹380 or less on its 7-day average AND the account is in offense.
- Never touch a winner except a budget raise of 20% at most, once every 3 days.
- How many campaigns fit: daily budget available (budget left / days left) / running daily spend.
- Change one variable per new campaign.

## Refresh when ads wear out (PROPOSED)
- Trigger: 7-day CAC above ₹380 (frequency and reach cost only explain why).
- Under ~30 purchases in the ad set: add 2 to 3 new ads into the same ad set (keeps its data).
- 30+ purchases: launch the new ads as a new campaign; switch off the old one once the new one is ahead.
- Ads that got almost no spend (under ~₹300 in a week): not judged; later tested in their own campaign.

## Retargeting (PROPOSED)
- Site visitors (14 days) and add-to-carts (7 days) only, 5 to 10% of budget, after ~1,000 visits.

## CAC stages: how CAC comes down (PROPOSED)
CAC does not fall on its own. Each stage needs a lever. Campaign 1 (WhatsApp funnel, no site, no reviews) was ~₹941.
| Stage | When | Expected CAC | Lever that moves it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Learning | Days 1 to 7 | ₹450 to ₹650 | None yet; Meta is finding buyers. Hold unless it breaks the stop rule. |
| 1 Cut | Week 2 | ₹380 to ₹450 | Pause losing ads, keep winners, start retargeting, fix the weakest site step |
| 2 Winners | Weeks 3 to 4 | ₹300 to ₹380 | New versions of the winning angle; stack a second campaign; partnership ads |
| 3 Trained | Month 2+ | ₹250 to ₹300 | Account has 100+ purchases; weekly creative refresh; 3-kit offer pushes kits per order |
Stop rule: 16 or fewer orders after ₹10,000 (CAC above ~₹600) means pause and fix before spending more.
