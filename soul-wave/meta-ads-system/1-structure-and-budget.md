# 1. Structure and Budget

Last updated: 23 Nov 2026.

## Rules
- Cold traffic only; the website carries trust (reviews, videos). (LOCKED)
- No views or awareness campaign on Meta; no retargeting of video viewers. Influencer work is separate and not counted for sales. (LOCKED)
- All budgets and CAC include GST. (LOCKED)
- Few ads with enough budget each, not many ads on a thin budget. (LOCKED)
- Grow by launching a new campaign with its own budget, not by editing a running one. (LOCKED)
- Exclude Mumbai (workshops cover it). South first, North added later. Don't let narrow pincodes push CAC up. (LOCKED)

## Setup (PROPOSED)
- Campaign 1: Sales, optimise for Purchase, ~₹2,000/day, ONE ad set, broad (target states, Mumbai excluded, age 25+, Advantage+ audience, no interests), 4 to 5 ads.
- Attribution: default 7-day click, 1-day view. Don't add engaged-view.
- Learning happens per ad set: budget change over ~20%, audience, optimisation event, or adding/removing ads restarts it.

## Stacking (PROPOSED)
- Launch the next campaign (new angle, offer or region, own budget) only when the running one holds target CAC for 3+ days.
- Never touch a winning campaign except a budget raise of 20% at most, once every 3 days.
- Budget cap decides how many campaigns fit: average daily limit = budget left / days left.
- Change one variable per new campaign (angle OR offer OR region), so the result is readable.

## Refresh when ads wear out (PROPOSED)
- Under ~30 purchases in the ad set: add 2 to 3 new ads into the same ad set (keeps its data).
- 30+ purchases: launch the new ads as a new campaign; switch off the old one when the new one is ahead.

## Retargeting (PROPOSED)
- Site visitors (14 days) and add-to-carts (7 days) only, 5 to 10% of budget, after ~1,000 visits.
