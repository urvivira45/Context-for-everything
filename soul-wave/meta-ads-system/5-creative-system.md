# 5. Creative System: Angles, Hooks and Scripts

Last updated: 23 Nov 2026. How to choose what goes into the ads. (PROPOSED unless marked)

## Build order for every ad
Concept → angle → visual format → hook → script.

## Angle testing rule (LOCKED, 24 Nov)
- One angle per ad. Choose angles from research + brand fit + own sales data, never on intuition.
- Losing ads (no sales or low-quality sales) are killed in the running campaign.
- A killed angle is retested once in the next campaign with different visuals. If it fails again, the angle is dropped completely.
- When an ad wins, find out whether the script or the visuals did it (change one at a time).

## Script change rule (LOCKED, 24 Nov)
- Start from Urvi's original scripts. Change only what is required, list each change with its reason, and get Urvi's approval before changing anything else. No rewrites without asking.

## Angle selection rule (LOCKED, 24 Nov)
- Only angles scoring 4+ out of 5 on outside research are used. Scores: beginner confidence 5, feeling during 4, joy of making 4, stress relief 4, pride 4, something of my own 3 (dropped).
- Every script follows AIDA or PAS; the first 3 seconds are the hook.

## Launch angles, Campaign 1 (LOCKED, 24 Nov)
1. Beginner confidence ("easy even if you've never made anything")
2. The feeling during (getting absorbed, mind going quiet)
3. Joy of making (top reason people craft: 82.1%)
4. Stress relief (kept despite the Brand Bible rule because Campaign 1's best ad, Day 10, led with stress; unknown whether script or visuals sold it)
Pride (score 4) optional as a fifth ad, Urvi to decide. Something of my own dropped (score 3). Held back: gift/heritage (later test). Not used as the lead: phone-free (Brand Bible: not a digital detox brand), pride/"I made this" (Brand Bible: don't lead with the output; can be proof at the end).

## Selecting angles
- Lead with desire, outcome and transformation (calm, me-time, finishing something to hang on the wall). These last longer.
- Use fear or urgency (stock left, deadlines) sparingly: they spike and burn out within about a week.
- Each ad in a batch must be a different angle AND a different format. Five edits of one video count as one ad.
- Every ad shows the product, the price and the offer clearly; the price filters out people who won't pay.
- The ad's promise must match the page it lands on.

## Formats to rotate
Talking head (founder), B-roll process (stitching close-ups), unboxing / what's inside, customer review, static image.

## Hooks
- The hook (first 3 seconds or first line) is ~80% of the ad. Script only the hook; keep the rest as bullet points so the founder sounds natural. (LOCKED: hook = 80%)
- Call out the right person, not everyone. Viral-style hooks pull craft-curious non-buyers (Campaign 1 Ad 3 lesson).
- Most of India doesn't know Punch Needle: open with identification or desire, not the product name. Price and offer shown before the end.
- 3-second test: a stranger can tell what is sold within 3 seconds.
- Hook types: identification, name the problem, desire/outcome, how-to, contradiction, proof/review, process curiosity, offer, founder story. Hook bank from research: `market-research.md`.
- The first 3 seconds decide the hook rate (measure 3). If hook rate is low, change only the first 3 seconds.
- Regional-language text overlays for non-metro South audiences: test as a variable.

## Scripts
- Structures: AIDA (attention, interest, desire, action) or PAS (problem, agitate, solution). AIDA drove most Campaign 1 sales. (LOCKED)
- Captions: longer copy in AIDA/PAS, short lines, everyday words, ends with a specific call to action ("Tap Shop Now, pick any 3 kits, pay for 2").
- Existing footage only for now. (LOCKED)
- Structure: hook (0 to 3 s), problem or desire, product and what's inside, proof (review), offer and price, call to action.

## Format plan (LOCKED)
- Campaign 1: reels only (9:16). Placements are automatic, so the same 9:16 reels also run as Story ads (full-screen between stories).
- Find 3 to 4 winning angles first; then make carousels, statics and more reels on those angles only.
- Growth loop: angles → winning ad → more ads on that angle → scale.
- Every reel highlights the sale (pick any 3, pay for 2). Wording "WhatsApp Support"; CTA to the website.

## Weekly production (PROPOSED)
- Launch new ads on one fixed day a week (best sales day). 3 to 5 new ads a week at this budget.
- Statics from the 7 product photos as a separate test; videos and statics in separate ad sets.
- Native-looking ads beat polished ones; show reviews in fresh ways (WhatsApp chat screenshot, voice note).

## When an angle wins
- Make new versions of that angle in new formats and with new hooks. Change one variable at a time.
- When it wears out, refresh per `1-structure-and-budget.md`.

## Naming (so the agent can read results)
Ad name = angle_format_hook-number_date (e.g. metime_broll_h1_2611).
