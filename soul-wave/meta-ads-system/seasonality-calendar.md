# Seasonality Calendar (India, Meta ad costs)

Last updated: 23 Nov 2026. Use with the launch planner. Evidence is thin for India after Diwali; replace with Soul Wave's own CPM data each month.

| Period | What happens | Effect on ads | Source / confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug to New Year | India's festive season runs about five months | Competition stays elevated | Meta newsroom (promotional) |
| 1 to 7 Nov 2026 | Week before Diwali | Costs rise | Singular (app installs, Android) |
| 8 to 15 Nov 2026 | Diwali peak | Peak spend | Singular |
| From ~23 Nov | Costs climb week by week; Black Friday 27 Nov and Cyber Monday growing in India | Expect higher CPMs into early Dec | Singular, Meta; medium |
| Final week before Diwali (any year) | CPMs +40 to 70% | Avoid tests | Earlier research |
| Big marketplace sales (Big Billion Days, Great Indian Festival, end-of-season sales) | Big brands raise bids | Avoid launching tests | Earlier research; medium |
| Christmas to New Year | Global spend peaks the week after Christmas | No India figure; watch own CPMs | Singular (global only); low |
| January | Global spend stays above mid-Nov into late Jan | No India figure | Singular (global only); low |

Rule (PROPOSED): don't launch new tests in the costliest weeks; hold winners (defense) and launch when costs ease (offense).

Sources: https://www.singular.net/festive-season-report-2026/ ; https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/how-black-friday-is-becoming-indias-new-shopping-moment/amp/
