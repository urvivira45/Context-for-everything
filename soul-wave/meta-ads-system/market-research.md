# Market Research: Buyers, Competitors, Hook Bank

Last updated: 23 Nov 2026. Desk research by Claude (not yet checked against Soul Wave's own customers). Sources at the end. Next step: add Soul Wave's own review and WhatsApp language, which beats any desk research.

## Who is buying hobbies in India and why
- Young urban adults are returning to hands-on hobbies: embroidery, knitting, crochet, pottery, painting, baking (Deccan Chronicle, May 2026).
- Reasons in their own words: "I started knitting because I was anxious all the time"; hobbies give "a private space where you can exist without evaluation"; free time has started to feel like something to justify; wanting hobbies with no pressure to earn from them; digital fatigue; loneliness and wanting connection (clubs, workshops).
- 33% of Gen Z in India say they feel stressed or anxious all or most of the time (Deloitte 2025, cited by Deccan Chronicle).
- Global data: searches for offline hobbies like embroidery up 136% over six months (Michaels report, cited).

## Punch Needle buyer language (mostly global, test in India)
- Desire: "The simple, repetitive act of stitching helps me wind down"; "scrolling on my phone does nothing to help me turn my mind off"; "mindfulness that involves movement"; "watching the design slowly come to life is so satisfying"; "the satisfying feeling of yarn looping through fabric"; "started for the art and stayed for the calm"; "I made it myself"; "part of my self-care routine".
- Pain: "another evening lost scrolling through your phone"; "feeling guilty about the time wasted"; "I quit after 20 minutes" (cheap kits); "most beginner craft results look like beginner craft".
- Discovery: buyers often don't know what Punch Needle is before trying ("wasn't even exactly sure what punch needling was"), then get hooked after the first piece.

## Objections to answer in ads and on the site
1. "I've never done any craft. Can I do this?" (beginner fear)
2. "How long does it take?" (time)
3. "Will it look good, or like a beginner's craft?" (result quality)
4. "Is everything in the box?" (hidden costs)
5. "Is the material good, or will it fray?" (quality)
6. "What if it arrives damaged / I don't like it?" (risk; Soul Wave: replacement with unboxing video)
7. "Why ₹999 when kits sell for ₹950 or less?" (price vs Craftmate and marketplace kits)
8. "Can I pay cash on delivery?" (Soul Wave is prepaid only; competitor Craftmate offers COD under ₹3,000)

## Angles to test (from the research)
1. Phone-free calm ("evenings back from the scroll")
2. "I made this" pride / wall art for your home
3. Zero experience needed (beginner proof)
4. Satisfying process (loops popping through fabric)
5. Hobby without pressure ("not a side hustle, just yours")
6. Gift you make yourself (secondary; Soul Wave is evergreen, not gifting-led)
7. Value: pick any 3, pay for 2
