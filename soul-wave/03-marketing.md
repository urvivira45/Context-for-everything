## Meta ads
- Kit ads and workshop ads run as separate campaigns.
- Kit campaign launch moved to ~16 to 18 Nov 2026 (new design prints delayed by Diwali factory closure).
- Targets: CAC goal ₹250 to ₹350. Budget planned for 500 sales at ₹250 CAC. ₹30k ad ceiling (GST-inclusive) for the first 100-order push. Ad budgets and CAC tracked GST-inclusive.

- Club welcome + club abandoned-cart merged into one email. 24h/48h abandoned-cart reminders already built.
- No workshop invites in sequences.
- Broad email marketing doesn't work in India. Email only to past buyers. WhatsApp is the main channel.
