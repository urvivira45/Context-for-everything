# Soul Wave Hiring: Content Creation Associates

Last updated: 17 Nov 2026. Source: hiring chat (30 Sep to 12 Nov 2026).

## Structure
- Two entry-level hires (0 to 1 year), both titled "Content Creation Associate": one Shooting, one Editing. Each is a generalist in a different domain: one creates the content, the other edits it and sorts out the rest.
- 3-month paid pilot project, not an internship. It continues as a full-time role with a raise if it goes well.
- Budget up to ₹20k/month for both.
- Pilot pay ₹8,000 to ₹10,000/month each, then ₹12,000 to ₹18,000/month if continued, with room to grow (previously discussed: ₹7,500 each, then ₹10,000 to 12,000 for shooting and ₹6,000 to 8,000 for editing).
- Both work in the office, Lower Parel. I do not want a remote hire I have to chase for updates; I want to see the work happen.
- Content strategy and ideas stay with me. The hires bring the making.
- JDs and roles should not be Punch Needle only; more art forms will come.

## Roles (as in the final JDs)
- **Shooting:** phone-first shooting (iPhone and ring light) of Reels, Shorts, ad footage and photos for Soul Wave and my personal brand, at the office and around Mumbai. Works from reference reels and shot lists. Builds simple setups with backgrounds, paper, fabric, lights and props. Captures hands making art, ASMR, close-ups, founder content and behind the scenes. Publishes to Instagram, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn per the calendar and logs views, likes, comments, shares and saves on a shared sheet (no analysis). Comes to workshops and events within Mumbai with me only when needed, not every time.
- **Editing:** edits Reels and short-form video in VN (clean pacing, cuts to the music, readable text, simple transitions, no heavy VFX). Makes the small editing calls. Gets several clips from every shoot. Cuts ads and organic from existing footage using my direction and copy. Researches songs and trending audio and gives 2 or 3 options per Reel. Keeps raw footage, drafts and finals organised. Publishes and logs numbers as above.
- **Both, as they settle in:** suggest content ideas from about 15 days once they know the brand, research trends and formats, and help reach out to creators using preset messaging.
- **Influencers, later:** they find influencers, send them to me (and AI) for approval, then message and negotiate barter collabs. The flow is already settled. To start, they research 10 to 20 influencers in about 2 hours from competitor accounts I give (for example Not Your Type) and judge if each fits the brand, so my time does not go into research.

## Terms
- Hours 11:00 AM to 7:30 PM, 30-minute lunch, 15-minute break.
- Alternating six- and five-day weeks: every Sunday off, alternate Saturdays off. Weekend workshop means a comp-off that week.
- Travel: a train pass from their nearest station to ours, nothing else.
- What they get: weekly one-hour one-to-one with me on a different part of social media and strategy, a view of how content is built to perform, an overview of the whole business including supply chain, and specific feedback on every piece.
- Apply with CV and portfolio (own work is fine) to soulwavekits@gmail.com.

## JD rules
- Explain what Soul Wave is before saying it is an entry-level role.
- Human but professional, not mechanical.
- No "Lala company" tone (expecting everything for a small salary). I want to give back to them, not eat them alive. No "not my job" lines, no lines that imply working beyond hours, no "must already understand everything" and no promise to teach in the opening.
- They need a knack and an eye for short-form (why a shot works, what makes content engaging), not expertise.
- Pay is the first line under "What you'll get".
- Final JDs made as two PDFs (Shooting, Editing).

## Hiring process
1. Invite email asks for a call slot only. It does not mention the task.
2. Confirmation email with the Google Meet link.
3. Interview call.
4. Task email right after the call. Two different tasks, one for shooting and one for editing. Submit by 6:00 PM the next day; I reply within 24 hours of submission.
   - Editing task: they use their own clips or stock clips from the internet and edit a video; I send a reference video.
   - Shooting task: must not be about a product or object, since their cameras differ. Brief not settled.
5. Decision email: a decline, or an acceptance inviting them to the office in person, with timings, leave policy and joining date.
- After they join, the SOP and brand documents must be ready so they can start working flawlessly.
