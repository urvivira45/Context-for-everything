# Soul Wave Workshops

Last updated: 17 Nov 2026. From the workshop chats (Jul to Nov 2026).

## Formats and names
- "Pick & Punch": Punch Needle workshop. "Stick & Sparkle" (later "Bedazzled Coasters"): tube glass beads stuck onto a wooden coaster with B-6000 glue. Rejected "Parlour" (salon feel) and "Beaded Coaster" (used by Churchgate).
- Done so far: 9 Punch Needle, 1 bedazzled. 67 bookings in total, Jul to Oct 2026.
- Session shape: needle threading and a small shape first so everyone understands the mechanism, then design and yarn choice, then the bigger project. Last 30 minutes covers fabric, yarn, other project types and ways to close a piece.
- Take-home: artwork, Punch Needle, hoop, plus extra yarn. Never claim people finish in the session.
- Standing teaching assets: a printed 3-hour workshop flow and a 6-part tutorial script (for the kit QR/YouTube). The flow includes a 5-minute mid-session break and a felt-backing finish so the piece works as a coaster. Default needle length size 8; brick-stitch for a clean finish.

## Ads
- Two ads in use: Ad 1 "POV: hours that actually feel like yours" (offer hook, names the workshop at second zero, proven converter) and Ad 2 "3 reasons to try Punch Needle with Soul Wave" (why us, not YouTube).
- Four were built; two were retired. The next ad comes from whichever reel outperforms organically, not from a new build.
- Always pair an offer-hook ad with a content ad. Never two content ads together.
- Five primary texts per ad, swapped per venue. Headlines "Limited Seats" or "Grab Your Seat". End card: Joining us? / Punch Needle Workshop / date / venue.
- Keep date and venue in the primary text. They filter the wrong area out and most feed views never reach the end card.
- One campaign per workshop when several run in a weekend, so each has its own CAC and can be paused alone.
- Pausing the weaker ad inside an ad set keeps the ad set's learning. Building a new ad set resets it, so never rebuild close to a workshop date.
- Change one variable at a time, and not on the same day as a budget change.
- Early setup rules: Advantage+ placements off, manual Reels/Stories, ~5km radius, craft interests + top-10% income, WhatsApp objective.
- Budget pattern used: ~₹1,400 per location (₹164/day x 7 days + GST), re-message existing leads first. Ad budgets are set ex-GST; plan against 18% on top.
- Use only our own samples and photos in ads, never another creator's.

## WhatsApp flow
- Cold leads: greeting that lets them pick a location (Meta mixes areas), location details with map link, founder intro, voice note for first-timers, advance ask, confirmation, day-before reminder, morning-of. Quick replies /2M /3V /4T style.
- Price goes in the first message on purpose. Someone who balks at the price costs nothing at message one and twenty minutes at message four.
- Follow-up order: testimonial video (mother-daughter one performs best), participant artwork, Google review, room shot, direct ask with seat count, soft close asking locality.
- Cadence: cold leads daily, warm alternate days. Six follow-ups is the ceiling.
- The permission pattern ("shall I...", "will it be okay if...") is core. Never tighten it out.
- The voice note for first-timers is the single best converting asset in the flow.
- Decline path asks which locality they are in, so the list cleans itself without asking anyone to be removed.
- Registration via Tally form; go/no-go rule before each date.
- Next step: move leads into a community group after the workshop season, so updates stop being individual messages.

## Lessons
- No weekday workshops (Malad Friday failed, Saturday fixed it).
- Need ~2 weeks lead time, not 5 days.
- Repeat venues are cheaper than new ones. First contact in a new area is the expensive part, and the cost drops the second time.
- Long weekends and festivals kill attendance (2 to 4 Oct). Judge a date by the festival calendar before booking.
- Conversion is the bottleneck, not traffic (100+ chats, 5 registrations in Oct). More budget on an unconverted pipeline does not fix it.
- CAC: ₹167 Aug, ₹228 average now, target ₹200.
- A festival day or a funding interruption distorts a day's ad numbers. Do not re-plan on one bad day.
