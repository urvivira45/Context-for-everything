# Soul Wave: Business Overview

Last updated: 12 Nov 2026

## What Soul Wave is
- D2C brand selling DIY Punch Needle kits across India, plus creative workshops in Mumbai and Thane. Scaling toward corporate gifting and corporate workshops.
- Website: soulwave.co.in (live end to end since 18 Oct 2026).
- Positioning (locked): **Soul Wave makes the joy of creating incredibly easy for complete beginners.** A frustration-free first creative experience.
- Differentiator: Amazon sells supplies, YouTube sells tutorials. Soul Wave gives matched, tested materials plus real support.
- Evergreen, not festival-led. For any bored moment or free weekend. Ad messaging should not hang on festivals or gifting calendars.
- Long term: more art forms beyond Punch Needle, each kept as its own separate craft on the site.

## Products
**Punch Needle kits (7 designs, all live since 9 Nov 2026):** Butterfly Bloom, You Got This, Mountain Escape, Dino-mite, Lovebug, Purrfectly, Cosmic Dreams. 8 products on the site: 7 singles + Combo.
- Product photos are my real stitched pieces, edited only for light and background. I stitch the kit designs myself.
- Single kit ₹999 (₹899 without needle, regular ₹1,099). Combo (any 2 different designs) ₹1,349 (₹1,249 without needle, regular ₹1,449). Super Combo is DROPPED permanently (no space in the box), never bring it back.
- Each separately ordered kit is complete with its own needle. A combo shares one needle.
- Kit item wording: "1 Adjustable Punch Needle", "3 Different Needles", "Printed Pattern Fabric". Scissors are NOT included (stated everywhere).
- One video tutorial covering Punch Needle at a basic level, in short chapters. NOT per design, NOT "beginner to advanced". Never claim otherwise.
- "Tested so it works": 2 to 3 months spent perfecting the needle, yarn and fabric combination. Say "there are chances" stitches come out with wrong combos, never "100%".

**Coming next:**

## Brand rules (always)
- "Punch Needle" always capitalised.
- No em dashes.
- No wellness, mindfulness or health claims in ads and product pages. No anti-screen language. Describe absorption through observable detail ("you'll forget to check your phone").
- No overclaiming finished outcomes (people take yarn home to finish).
- Understating scarcity is fine, inflating it is not.
- No fabricated reviews.
- Exception I decided: the About Us page keeps "Stress is a problem, but managing it shouldn't be" and the Impact section. Do not re-flag.
