# Soul Wave: Hard-Won Learnings

Read before revisiting pricing, ads or growth plans.

- **Combo share drives profit.** Break-even CAC ~₹409 on singles, ~₹655 on combos. Combos above 40% of orders make realistic CAC profitable.
- **Workshops are the only consistently near-profitable channel.** Corporate workshops are highest margin.
- **Trust gap, not price gap.** Campaign 1 failed at the prepaid checkout moment, not on price or ad quality. Pack-first video (filming the packing with the customer's name before payment) closed that gap.
- **Proven baseline:** Campaign 1 sold 10 kits in one week from a cold start. Use ~10 kits/week as baseline.
- **Tier-2 moat is supply scarcity, not affluence,** but many tier-2/3 leads couldn't afford the kit. Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, Jaipur, Indore leads dropped off heavily.
- **Affluence proxies (iPhone, high-income filters) don't work in India.**
- **Match creative to audience.** A viral clip in an ad pulls craft-curious people regardless of income targeting.
- **Retargeting only helps when there are new products.** Warm leads go to WhatsApp, not paid ad sets.
- **Solo execution slips** (influencer outreach, landing page). That's structural; the hire is the fix.
- **Sequence discipline:** each product rung funds the next. Don't skip steps.
- **Corporate Diwali decisions happen in September.** Build that pipeline before October.
- **Holiday weekends kill Mumbai workshops** (Gandhi Jayanti long weekend: Versova got zero bookings from 55+ chats, plus ad funds ran out mid-campaign).
- **City-level lead data was never recorded in Campaign 1.** Three sales in a city is a faint signal, not proof. Record city and pincode from now on.
- **Soul Wave is evergreen,** not gifting or festival-led. Lead with the joy of making.
- **Festivals raise ad costs** (e.g. Big Billion Days). Don't compete in the festive window on a small budget.
