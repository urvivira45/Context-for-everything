## Ad 4: Gifting, "Gift a little creative time" (AIDA), text overlay + music, ~16s. LOCKED 27 Nov
| Stage | On screen | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | Here's a gift that's not chocolates or another photo frame 🎁 | Hands holding the kit box, clearly a gift |
| Interest | Instead, gift them something new to create 🪡 | Box opens, contents revealed |
| Interest | A little break for the one who never takes one 🤍 | A busy person (any gender) finally sitting down with the hoop |
| Interest | And a little reminder of you, in every stitch | Close-up of a stitch going in |

Decisions (Urvi): general gifting, not Secret Santa or Christmas; gender neutral; no comparison line against chocolates in Interest; idea = gifting a busy person a little time. Generic chocolates/frames only in visuals, no brand logos.
Evidence: experiential gifts make recipients feel closer to the giver (Chan and Mogilner, Wharton/U of Toronto); chocolates, photo frames, mugs top Indian gift orders (MyFlowerTree; Instamart Mother's Day 2026); Campaign 1: 3 of 10 buyers were gift or heritage, untargeted.
