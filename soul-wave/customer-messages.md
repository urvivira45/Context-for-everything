# Customer emails, WhatsApp and product descriptions

Last updated: 12 Nov 2026

Full message copy: "Soul Wave Customer Journey: Final Rules and Sequence" doc (Claude Docs, 6 Nov 2026).

## Message jobs
- Before purchase: E8 club welcome (Buy 2 Get 1), A1 1h (emotion, no offer), A2 24h (beginner doubts, no offer), A3 72h (Buy 2 Get 1, subject "Is this for me? (Yes, and your third kit can be free)").
- After purchase: E1/W1 confirm, E2/W2 dispatch + unboxing video ask, E3/W3 delivery and support (helpline box, unboxing claim within 3 days, no coupon), E4 thank-you + review + ₹150 coupon (subject "A little thank-you from us 💛"), W4 gift question + coupon, W5 to W8 weekly check-ins, E5/W9 coupon reminder on day 75 if unused (E5 also shows Buy 2 Get 1).
- E6 refund: "processed through Razorpay and takes 5 to 7 working days". E7 sign-in code.
- Email and matching WhatsApp do the same job in different form; WhatsApp is short.
- All WhatsApps sent by hand from WordPress.

## Rules
- Buy 2 Get 1 is the welcome offer; ₹150 coupon is the after-purchase thank-you. Never combined; only one line saying so in E4/E5.
- Offer wording: "Add any 3 kits to your bag and one is on us. No code needed. The offer is applied automatically at checkout." Do not say "lowest priced" in emails.
- Combo = 1 kit. 3 Combos: 1 Combo free. 2 Combos + 1 Single: Single free.
- No unboxing video ask in E1/W1. No "forward this email" gift lines (tutorials and support are physical cards). Gift question only in W4.
- One contact line in every email footer: "Questions? Reply to this email or WhatsApp us (clickable). We reply the same day, Monday to Saturday, 11 AM to 7 PM. Messages that come in late in the evening or outside these hours get a reply the next working day." Same line on website (with email instead of reply).
- No phone calls. No "24 to 48 hours" reply line.
- Abandoned carts greet by first name, fallback "there". Edit in plugin, not theme.
- Complaint within 24h of E3 holds E4, W4 and check-ins until resolved.
- E4 opening line doubles as an indirect nudge to open the kit; no direct reminder line.
- Days 28 to 75 covered by retargeting and new-design launch messages, not this sequence.
- Asking to share a customer's piece: manual reply only, never automated.
- Repeat buyers (E4 without coupon): parked until after first cycle.
- Shipping is free across India.
