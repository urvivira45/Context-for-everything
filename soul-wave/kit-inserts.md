Printed pieces that go inside every kit box. Canva file: "Soul Wave Resources".

## What goes in the box
- Founder's letter (A Little Note From Me), with Urvi's photo bottom right.
- Tangled in Trouble card: help is now via WhatsApp.

## Founder's letter: decisions
- Voice: warm and human, like a friend saying hello, not a brand. Opens with Urvi's own greeting, "Hey, how are you doing?"
- Core idea: making something just because you want to, no pressure to be good at it. Matches the website founder note and LinkedIn About.
- Story: art since school classes, then noticed everything she made had become for work or a deadline, started saying yes to new things again (learning to ski, new art forms), not good at all of them (dancing joke kept).
- Does not say she just started exploring art.
- Speaks to both first-timers and people who want to go deeper into the craft.
- No feedback or review ask in the letter. Reviews are asked in E4 and W7 instead.
- No "wonky stitches" line.
- Left-aligned text, not justified.
- Bold lines: "you don't have to be good at something to enjoy making it.", "A little space to make something with your hands, just because you want to.", "please send me a picture."
- Sign-off: "Happy creating, Urvi, Soul Wave 🌊".

## Founder's letter: final text
Hey, how are you doing?

I'm Urvi, the human behind Soul Wave, and I'm really glad this little box found its way to you.

Art has always been a part of my life, right from school art classes. But a while ago, I noticed that almost everything I made had become for something, whether it was work or a deadline. I had stopped making things just because I felt like it.

So I started saying yes to new experiences again, whether it was learning to ski or picking up a new art form. I wasn't good at everything I tried (dancing and I are still not on speaking terms 😅). But I had so much fun, and that's when it clicked for me: **you don't have to be good at something to enjoy making it.**

That's really what Soul Wave is about. **A little space to make something with your hands, just because you want to.** We started with Punch Needle, but there's so much more I'd love to bring your way.

Whether this is your very first time or you're looking to explore the craft further, we're here to help. Our step-by-step tutorials will guide you through the process, and if you feel stuck anywhere, just WhatsApp us. We'll be happy to help!

And when you finish, **please send me a picture.** I'd genuinely love to see how yours turned out!

Thank you for being a part of Soul Wave.

Happy creating,
Urvi
Soul Wave 🌊
