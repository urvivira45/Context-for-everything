## Where we stand
- Demand is proven: strangers bought from cold ads, workshops fill, one buyer found us through ChatGPT. The work now is distribution and bringing cost down, not proving the idea.
- Sales to mid Sep 2026: 67 kit and workshop sales, of which 7 from friends and family.

## Sales channels
1. Kits (D2C), the only channel that depends on ad CAC.
2. Workshops, B2C, the most reliable near-profitable channel.
3. Corporate: workshops lead. Corporate gifting needs more SKUs and gift packaging (not a brown box), so it is not the lead offer yet.

5. Professional referral: psychologists and wellness practitioners suggesting the kit as a creative activity, never as treatment.

## Strategy decisions
- Build owned audience (personal brand Instagram and YouTube, LinkedIn, Soul Wave Instagram) instead of relying only on rented reach. Keep ads running at a low budget while owned channels build. Owned channels take time (could be about 6 months), so ads stay on.
- Combo-first: ads, creators and the website push combos over singles. A combo shares one needle, so the combo story is not "one for you, one for a friend".
- Creators are for buzz only, never a sales source. No organic kit sales in forecasts.
- Kit ads and workshop ads are separate campaigns with separate goals.
- Paid ads: one campaign, scaled in budget phases, never restarted from zero per phase.
- Launch day: 20 creatives at launch (my call). Start at about ₹1,000/day, scale toward ~₹7,000/day.
- Tracking agent connected to Meta, pulling and analysing data about every 2 hours.
- Do not run workshops or ads into festival windows (Ganpati, Navratri, Diwali). Corporates do not book art workshops around festivals either.
- Kids' vacation window (Jan to Jun) has few long festivals: plan kid-friendly kits for it.

## Product roadmap

- 7 Punch Needle designs live (Nov 2026).
